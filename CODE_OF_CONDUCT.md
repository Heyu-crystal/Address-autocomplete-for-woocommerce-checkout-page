# Code of conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

Everyone is welcome here, whatever their age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation. We act and speak in ways that keep this community open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive and healthy.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind, patient and respectful, including when you disagree.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the people using the plugin.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.

## Not acceptable

- Sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when someone represents the project elsewhere.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers (HeyU Jewellery) through a [private report on GitHub](https://github.com/heyu-crystal/address-autocomplete-for-wp-woocommerce-checkout-page/security/advisories/new) or to the repository owner. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code and issues that do not follow this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour is harmful, following the [Contributor Covenant enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
